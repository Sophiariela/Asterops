The Aster interface language.

The Aster product palette and Geist type pairing, carried on Nocturne's structural foundations — a dense, near-black interface where the indigo accent marks a line, a state, or one filled action, never a flood. Every screen in this prototype draws from what's on this page.

Neutral ramp — 100 to 900
Accent ramp — 100 to 900

Dark steps (700–900) tint fills, hovers, and borders on this near-black ground; light steps (100–300) carry text on those tints. The accent floods exactly two things — the single primary action, and the one saturated section band. Everywhere else it is a line, a chip border, or a focus ring.

07 · Motion & interaction
Micro (hover, press)

120–160ms, ease-out. Color and border only — nothing moves.

Panels & overlays

200–240ms, ease-out. Opacity plus a small position or height change.

Page transitions

160–200ms fade. No slides or zooms between pages.

Restraint

No bounce, elastic, or spring easing. No motion without a state change behind it.

220ms ease-out

Panels, dropdowns, and expanding content move on this curve — quick enough to feel immediate, slow enough to track.
